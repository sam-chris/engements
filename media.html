Questions and Answers

1. What are the positives and negatives of hosting your videos on YouTube?

The positives are that YouTube is free, easy to use, and lets people watch your videos without using your website's storage. The negatives are ads, distractions, and having less control over your videos.

2. What is the most useful thing you can do with a transform?

The most useful thing is changing how an element looks or where it is placed. You can move, rotate, resize, or skew an element.

3. What is the difference between a transform and a transition?

A transform changes the position, size, or rotation of an element. A transition makes changes happen smoothly over time instead of instantly.
