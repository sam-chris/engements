My Hero: Johnny Depp

Interests | Hero

Johnny Depp is an actor who has appeared in many popular movies. I like him because he has played many different characters and has a unique style of acting. Some of his most famous characters are very different from each other.

Captain Jack Sparrow
Johnny Depp played Captain Jack Sparrow in the Pirates of the Caribbean movies. Jack Sparrow is a funny and unusual pirate who is always getting into trouble.
Edward Scissorhands
Johnny Depp played Edward in Edward Scissorhands. Edward is a quiet character who has scissors instead of normal hands.
Willy Wonka
Johnny Depp played Willy Wonka in Charlie and the Chocolate Factory. Willy Wonka owns a strange and amazing chocolate factory.
