3. What are special entity characters?

Special entity characters are codes used in HTML to display characters that have a special meaning in HTML or characters that are difficult to type. For example:

  • &lt; displays <
  • &gt; displays >
  • &amp; displays &
  • &copy; displays ©

4. What are phrase tags?

Phrase tags are HTML tags that give meaning or emphasis to text. Examples include <strong> for important text, <em> for emphasized text, and <mark> for highlighted text.

5. What is the difference between OL and UL Tags?

The <ol> tag creates an ordered list, where items are numbered. The <ul> tag creates an unordered list, where items normally appear with bullet points.

Ordered List (OL)

  1. First item
  2. Second item
  3. Third item

Unordered List (UL)

  • First item
  • Second item
  • Third item
