The Benefits of Exercise

Final Project Proposal

Name: Sam Nadeem

1. Title and Topic

My website title is The Benefits of Exercise. My website will explain how exercise helps people stay healthy and feel better.

2. Why I Chose This Topic

I chose this topic because exercise is important for everyone. I want to show how exercise can improve your health, mood, and energy. I also want to share easy ways people can stay active.

3. Content for My Website

Home Page

Introduction to exercise, why exercise is important, and pictures of different activities.

Page 1: Types of Exercise

Running, walking, swimming, biking, weight training, and stretching.

Page 2: Physical Benefits

Stronger muscles, better heart health, and more energy.

Page 3: Mental Benefits

Reducing stress, improving mood, and building confidence.

Page 4: Exercise Table

A table showing different exercises, how long people do them, which body parts they use, and their benefits.

Page 5: Exercise Tips

Drinking water, setting fitness goals, exercising regularly, and finding activities you enjoy.

4. Form and Table Information

Form

My form will ask visitors these questions:

1. What is your age?

2. How often do you exercise?

3. What is your favorite exercise?

4. How long do you exercise?

5. Do you enjoy exercising?

Table

My table will include different types of exercise, the time spent exercising, body parts used, and the benefits of each exercise.

5. How I Will Collect Information

I will collect information from health and fitness websites. I will write everything in my own words. I will use pictures that I take myself or pictures that are free to use. I will also give credit to my sources.

References

I have read and understand all the warnings. I will not copy information from other websites or use copyrighted pictures without permission. I will make my own HTML, CSS, and content. I will give credit to my sources and follow the honor code.
